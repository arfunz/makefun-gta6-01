A 3D open-world city game set in Kota Senja, a sun-soaked coastal city at
sunset. You are Raka, a courier who owes money to the Harbor King. Take on
5 numbered missions — deliver packages, collect money, and pay off the debt
to become the RAJA SENJA (King of Senja). Walk the streets or steal a car,
dodge traffic and pedestrians, and watch out for the police: every crime
raises your wanted level, and the police chase you down.

## How to play

- **WASD:** walk / drive · **Mouse:** camera
- **E:** enter / exit a car · **F:** punch · **Click:** shoot
- **Shift:** run · **P:** pause · **R:** restart · **M:** sound
- Complete all 5 missions to pay off the debt and win.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. All art and sound are
drawn and synthesized in code — no external files, no network.
