KOTA SENJA is a 3D open-world city game set in Kota Senja, a sun-soaked coastal city glowing in the sunset.
You play Raka, a courier who owes money to the Harbor King.
Five numbered missions take you across the city: deliver packages, collect money, and pay off the debt step by step.
Clear them all and Raka becomes the RAJA SENJA — the King of Senja.
Walk the streets on foot or steal a car and drive through traffic, past crowds of pedestrians.
The police chase you when you break the law, and every crime raises your wanted level.

## How to play

- WASD to walk or drive
- Mouse for the camera
- E to enter or exit a car
- F to punch
- Click to shoot
- Shift to run
- P to pause
- R to restart
- M for sound

Complete all 5 missions to pay off the debt and win.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. All art and sound are drawn and synthesized in code — no external files, no network.
